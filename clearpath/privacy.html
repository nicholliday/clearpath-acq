Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Clear Path Property Solutions Inc ("Clear Path," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, phone number, email, and the areas or properties you're interested in.
  • Records of our calls, texts, and emails with you, including call recordings where allowed by law.
  • Basic website data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To send you properties that match what you buy, and to follow up about them.
  • To answer your questions, schedule walkthroughs, and complete transactions.
  • To keep our records and meet legal obligations.

Text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties. We only share information with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our phone carrier), and only to provide that service.

You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. See our SMS Terms.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with title companies, closing attorneys, or other parties to a transaction you take part in, with service providers who work for us, or when required by law.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.
